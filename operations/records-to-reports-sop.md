# Records-to-Reports — Delivery Standard Operating Procedure

## Purpose
Deliver consistent, traceable reports from customer-supplied records without silently changing uncertain information.

## 1. Accept and scope
1. Complete the Customer Intake and Scope Sheet.
2. Confirm the customer has authority to share the data and authorises the processing.
3. Confirm record type, reporting period, file/row limits, calculations, deliverables, fee, payment, revision and delivery date.
4. Decline or rescope work involving unsupported audit, tax, legal or certification claims.

## 2. Receive and protect data
1. Use an access-controlled transfer and working location.
2. Do not use this public GitHub repository for customer data.
3. Minimise personal information; ask the customer to mask fields not needed.
4. Record receipt, file names, access permissions and agreed deletion date.
5. If unauthorised or unexpected sensitive data appears, stop and contact the customer.

## 3. Intake and preparation
1. Preserve original files unchanged.
2. Confirm format, reporting period, row count and required columns.
3. Ask about ambiguous fields, duplicate rules and category mappings.
4. Do not infer missing values or silently discard questionable rows.
5. Use the automation only for supported CSV transaction records until a new input type is separately tested.

## 4. Generate
1. Prepare a working CSV using the agreed column definitions.
2. Run the report engine following the repository README instructions.
3. Inspect the Data Issues sheet and command output.
4. Resolve issues with customer confirmation or disclose them clearly.
5. Do not issue final reports while material unresolved issues are hidden.

## 5. Quality assurance
- Reconcile input row count to valid, excluded and flagged records.
- Recalculate totals independently.
- Confirm report period, currency and organisation label.
- Confirm Excel totals match PDF totals.
- Open both files and inspect readability.
- Check that no sample/demo labels or unrelated customer information remain.
- Record the checks and reviewer/date.

## 6. Customer review and handover
1. Provide draft outputs for one consolidated correction round.
2. Correct errors within the agreed scope; quote separately for new analysis or expanded work.
3. Deliver final Excel, PDF and a short explanation of assumptions and limitations.
4. Obtain confirmation of receipt.
5. Record actual hours, rework, customer feedback and whether the fee was commercially viable.

## 7. Retention and closure
Delete working copies on the agreed date, subject to applicable legal obligations. Keep only the minimum business records needed for invoicing, scope and engagement history. Never retain customer source records merely because they may be useful later.
