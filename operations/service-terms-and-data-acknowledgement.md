## 1. Parties and service

These terms apply between **PNG Digital Intelligence** ("Provider") and **[Customer legal name]** ("Customer") for the Records-to-Reports service described in the accepted quote **[quote number/date]**.

The Provider will process the records supplied by the Customer and prepare only the outputs listed in the accepted quote.

## 2. Scope and changes

The accepted quote controls the reporting period, file and row limits, outputs, price, delivery target and correction round. Work outside that scope requires a written variation and an agreed additional fee and timeframe before the extra work starts.

The Provider may pause work where inputs are unreadable, materially incomplete, inconsistent, apparently unauthorised or outside the agreed scope.

## 3. Customer responsibilities and authority

The Customer confirms that:
- It is authorised to provide the records and request the agreed processing.
- It has informed relevant people and obtained permissions or other authority required for the intended sharing and processing.
- Its instructions, categories, definitions and reporting rules are accurate to the best of its knowledge.
- It will not knowingly provide information it has no right to disclose.
- It will review draft outputs and promptly identify errors or missing context.

The Customer remains responsible for the original records, business decisions and any statutory reporting or accounting obligations.

## 4. Processing and limitations

The Provider will take reasonable care to apply the agreed processing steps and check calculations against the supplied data. The Provider does not audit, independently verify, certify or guarantee the source records. A report may contain errors or omissions present in the source material or arising from ambiguous instructions. The Customer must review the outputs before relying on them.

The service is not an audit, assurance engagement, bank reconciliation, tax service, legal service, certified bookkeeping service or substitute for professional advice.

## 5. Fees, payment and delivery

The fee and payment milestones are those in the accepted quote. The delivery target begins only after the Provider has received complete, readable inputs, necessary explanations, accepted scope and any agreed initial payment. Final release/payment conditions must be stated in the quote. Extra work is not authorised by silence or informal assumptions.

## 6. Confidentiality and access

Each party will use the other party's confidential information only for this engagement and restrict access to people who need it for the agreed work. The Provider will not intentionally publish customer records or use them in public demonstrations, public repositories or unrelated projects.

The parties must agree a suitable transfer and working method before sensitive or confidential records are sent. The Provider must not claim encryption, certification, access controls or security capabilities unless those controls are actually implemented and verified.

## 7. Data minimisation, retention and deletion

The Customer should provide only the information needed for the service and mask or remove unnecessary sensitive personal information. The parties will record the approved transfer method, authorised users, retention period and deletion date below.

At the end of the agreed retention period, the Provider will delete working copies under its control, subject to any agreed return requirement and applicable legal recordkeeping obligations. Deletion from backups or third-party systems may depend on those providers' retention processes; do not promise immediate deletion from systems outside the Provider's control.

If information appears unauthorised, unexpectedly sensitive or unrelated to the agreed purpose, the Provider may stop processing, restrict access and contact the Customer for instructions.

## 8. Drafts, corrections and acceptance

The quote includes one consolidated correction round limited to correcting outputs against the agreed source records and instructions. New records, changed definitions, new reporting questions or expanded deliverables are additional work. The Customer is responsible for final review and acceptance.

## 9. Intellectual property and reuse

The Customer retains rights in its source records. The Provider retains its pre-existing templates, generic methods and software. The Customer may use the paid final deliverables for its internal business purposes, subject to any third-party rights in source materials. Any broader publication, resale or redistribution arrangement must be agreed separately in writing.

## 10. Disputes and governing law

The parties should first attempt to resolve concerns through good-faith discussion between their nominated representatives. Any governing law, jurisdiction, liability allocation, termination rights and dispute process must be reviewed and completed by a PNG legal practitioner before this document is used as a binding agreement.
