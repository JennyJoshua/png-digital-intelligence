## 4. Observations

- The register contains ten entries across three income records and seven expense records.
- The largest expense category in this sample is venue hire at K600.00.
- All sample income records are marked Confirmed and all expense records are marked Paid.
- The supplied sample contains no opening balance, bank statement, budget or supporting receipts, so cash position, budget variance and transaction validity cannot be assessed.

## 5. Data quality and limitations

- The dataset is synthetic and intentionally small.
- No duplicate references are present in the sample.
- Dates and amounts are consistently formatted.
- The report assumes the supplied rows belong to the stated period.
- No independent source documents were checked.
- The net figure is a simple calculation: listed income minus listed expenses. It is not a closing bank or cash balance.
