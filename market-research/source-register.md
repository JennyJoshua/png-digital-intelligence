## Source handling standards

For every new source, capture:
- Publisher or organisation.
- Exact URL and page title.
- Date accessed and, where available, publication/update date.
- Specific claim supported.
- Whether the source is first-party, government, independent media or user-generated.
- Reuse/licensing terms and privacy implications.
- Confidence and verification status.

## Data reuse rule

Publicly viewable does not mean freely reusable or resellable. Before copying, transforming or commercialising data, inspect the applicable licence, copyright notice, terms of use, privacy notice and any relevant legal restrictions. When unclear, seek permission or exclude the material.
