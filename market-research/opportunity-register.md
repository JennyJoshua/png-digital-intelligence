## Priority recommendation

### First: records-to-reports pilot (OPP-01)
This is the recommended first commercial test because it can be delivered as a small, clearly scoped service without building a platform. Use customer-authorised sample data, measure effort and obtain feedback.

### Second: one sector intelligence sample (OPP-02)
Choose the sector only after customer discovery. Possible areas include construction/infrastructure, agriculture/fisheries, education suppliers or NGO/development projects. Do not collect or resell data until reuse rights are checked.

### Third: RAG preparation discovery (OPP-03)
Offer a defined preparation service: source inventory, extraction/OCR, cleaning, deduplication, metadata, chunking, citations and update plan. Be explicit that data preparation is distinct from building or hosting a complete chatbot.

## Scoring method for later prioritisation

Score each opportunity from 1 (low) to 5 (high) on:
- Confirmed customer pain.
- Willingness to pay.
- Ease of reaching buyers.
- Repeatability.
- Delivery feasibility with current tools.
- Lawful access to required data.

Subtract risk where data rights, privacy, accuracy or delivery complexity are unresolved. Do not treat the initial qualitative assessment above as a validated score.

## Evidence required before building

For each opportunity, record:
- Customer role and organisation type.
- Current method or provider.
- Time, money or risk caused by the problem.
- Frequency of the problem.
- Existing budget or spending behaviour.
- Requested deliverable and acceptable turnaround.
- Whether the customer will pay for a pilot.
- Data access and permission conditions.

## Decision rule

Do not build a full software product solely because the idea sounds useful. First obtain evidence of a real problem, a reachable buyer, lawful data access and a credible path to payment.
