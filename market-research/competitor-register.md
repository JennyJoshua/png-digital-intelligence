# PNG Market and Competitor Register

**Version:** 1.0  
**Research snapshot:** 18 November 2026  
**Evidence type:** Publicly advertised services and public web information  
**Status:** Initial desk research; not a verified market-share study

## How to read this register

The organisations below are potential competitors, substitutes or adjacent providers. Inclusion means that public material indicates a relevant offering; it does not establish current trading activity, market share, customer satisfaction or the full scope of their operations. Pricing is recorded only where publicly observed and should be rechecked before commercial decisions.

## Competitive position recommended

Compete through a combination of:
- PNG-specific, carefully sourced and maintained information.
- Data cleaning and conversion for organisations with existing records.
- Clear provenance, dates, definitions and quality notes.
- Small, affordable, measurable deliverables.
- Sector-specific knowledge collections rather than generic directories or chatbots.
- Ongoing update and reporting services where customers demonstrate recurring need.

## Research limitations and next actions

1. Verify each provider's current service offering and whether it is actively accepting customers.
2. Record direct customer experiences only when obtained lawfully and with permission.
3. Recheck advertised prices and terms before using them in proposals.
4. Interview target customers to understand unmet needs and existing suppliers.
5. Identify partnership opportunities; not every adjacent provider is a direct competitor.
6. Do not infer that a market is empty because a web search did not find a provider.
