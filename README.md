# PNG Digital Intelligence

**Turning scattered information into valuable intelligence.**

PNG Digital Intelligence is a proposed Papua New Guinea-focused data, research and digital knowledge services venture. It will help organisations turn scattered information, manual records and institutional knowledge into organised, reliable and usable digital assets.

## Initial service areas

1. **Data intelligence and research** — structured datasets, source-verified research, sector intelligence and data preparation.
2. **Business automation and reporting** — data cleaning, records-to-reports services, recurring management reports and practical workflow automation.
3. **Digital knowledge infrastructure** — document and archive preparation, searchable knowledge collections and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) data preparation.

## Repository purpose

This repository is the venture's business control and intelligence workspace. It holds strategy, market evidence, opportunity assessments, product definitions, validation plans and operating procedures.

## Working principles

- Start with customer problems and evidence of willingness to pay.
- PNG-first, with potential to serve Pacific markets later.
- Prefer reusable products and recurring services over one-off custom work alone.
- Use free or low-cost tools until customer revenue justifies upgrades.
- Verify source quality, dates, reuse rights and limitations before selling data.
- Never publish confidential client records or personal information in this public repository.
- Distinguish verified facts from estimates, hypotheses and recommendations.

## Current stage

**Stage: market validation and service design.** No market demand, pricing, revenue or customer commitments should be treated as proven until validated directly.
